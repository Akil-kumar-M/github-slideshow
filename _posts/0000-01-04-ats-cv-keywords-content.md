title: "ATS-Friendly CV – Keywords & Content Tips"
---

- **Mirror the Job Description (JD):** Copy exact keywords from the JD into your CV (skills, tools, technologies, role titles)
- **Use Both Acronym & Full Form:** Write *Artificial Intelligence (AI)*, *Search Engine Optimization (SEO)* – ATS may search either variant
- **Quantify Achievements:** Replace vague statements with numbers (e.g., "Reduced page load time by 40%", "Managed team of 8 engineers")
- **Strong Action Verbs:** Start every bullet with an action verb – *Developed*, *Optimized*, *Led*, *Implemented*, *Designed*, *Delivered*
- **Skills Section:** List tools, technologies, methodologies, and domain knowledge explicitly (e.g., Python, REST APIs, Agile, AWS EC2)
- **Tailor Per Application:** Adjust your headline, summary, and top skills for each specific role – do not use a one-size-fits-all CV
- **Avoid Keyword Stuffing:** Integrate keywords naturally into experience bullets, not in hidden text or white font
- **Professional Summary:** Open with a 3–5 line paragraph that includes your job title, years of experience, top 2–3 skills, and a career goal
- **Contact Details:** Include LinkedIn profile URL and GitHub / portfolio link where relevant
- **Avoid Abbreviations ATS May Not Know:** Spell out niche internal tool names or company-specific acronyms
