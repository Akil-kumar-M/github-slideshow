**Naukri Profile (complete these to reach 100% profile strength)**

- ✅ Upload updated resume (PDF, < 2 MB)
- ✅ Set a role-specific headline
- ✅ Fill in current CTC, expected CTC, and notice period
- ✅ Add all relevant skills (15+ tags)
- ✅ Set job preferences: preferred locations, job type, industry, function

**CV Improvements for ATS**

- ✅ Single-column layout with no tables or text boxes
- ✅ Section headings match ATS keywords (*Work Experience*, *Education*, *Skills*)
- ✅ Every bullet point starts with an action verb and includes a measurable outcome
- ✅ Skills section lists technologies, tools, and domain terms matching target JDs
- ✅ Dates are consistent throughout (MM/YYYY format)
- ✅ LinkedIn URL and GitHub / portfolio added to contact section
- ✅ File saved as `.docx` or ATS-safe `.pdf` with a professional filename

**Quick Wins**

- ✅ Run your CV through a free ATS checker (e.g., Jobscan, Resume Worded)
- ✅ Apply to a role within 24–48 hours of posting for higher recruiter visibility on Naukri
- ✅ Enable "Open to Opportunities" on both Naukri and LinkedIn
