- **Personal Details:** Full name, date of birth, gender, marital status, current city / home town
- **Contact Info:** Active mobile number & professional email address
- **Resume Upload:** Updated PDF resume (max 2 MB recommended)
- **Profile Photo:** Clear, professional headshot (optional but improves visibility)
- **Headline:** 1–2 line role-specific tagline (e.g., "Senior Java Developer | 5 Years of Experience | Spring Boot & AWS")
- **Summary:** 3–5 sentences covering your experience, key skills, and career goal
- **Work Experience:** Company name, designation, start & end dates, key responsibilities
- **Education:** Degree, institution, year of passing, percentage / CGPA
- **Key Skills:** Minimum 10–15 relevant technical & functional skills (used for search match)
- **Certifications / Courses:** Name, issuing organisation, year
- **Languages Known:** English proficiency level matters for most roles
- **Salary Details:** Current CTC, expected CTC, notice period
