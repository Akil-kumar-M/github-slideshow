title: "ATS-Friendly CV – Formatting Rules"
---

- **File Format:** Submit as **.docx** or **ATS-safe PDF** – avoid scanned PDFs
- **Font & Size:** Use standard fonts (Calibri, Arial, Times New Roman) at 10–12 pt; avoid decorative fonts
- **No Tables / Text Boxes:** ATS parsers skip content inside tables and text boxes
- **No Headers / Footers:** Keep contact info in the body – headers and footers are often ignored by ATS
- **No Columns:** Use a single-column layout; two-column layouts confuse most parsers
- **No Graphics / Icons:** Remove logos, icons, charts, and images – ATS cannot read them
- **Standard Section Headings:** Use recognised labels – *Summary*, *Work Experience*, *Education*, *Skills*, *Certifications*
- **Consistent Date Format:** Use MM/YYYY or Month YYYY throughout (e.g., Jan 2022 – Mar 2024)
- **Bullet Points:** Use simple hyphens ( – ) or filled circles; avoid fancy symbols or wingding characters
- **File Name:** Save as `FirstName_LastName_Role_Resume.pdf` for easy identification
- **Page Limit:** 1 page for < 5 years experience; 2 pages maximum for senior profiles
